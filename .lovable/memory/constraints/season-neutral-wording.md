---
name: Season-neutral owner wording
description: Owner screens/emails must never say "warm", "warmer" or "cold" — season-neutral wording only (Jo, 5 Oct)
type: constraint
---
Owner-facing screens, emails and SMS must not use "warm", "warmer" or "cold" (it's summer; keep wording season-neutral).

Approved replacements:
- "Your warmer home is on its way" → "Your comfier home is on its way"
- "Your home is warmer" → "Your home is insulated"
- "Two visits, then you're warm" → "Two visits, then you're done"
- "Know someone with a cold house?" → "Know someone who'd love this?"

Apply to any new owner-facing copy too. Does not apply to trade agreements ("cold calling" legal wording) or climate-region data ("Cold" zone).
