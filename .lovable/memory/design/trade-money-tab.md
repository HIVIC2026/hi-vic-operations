Jo-approved trade "Money" tab for electricians AND installers. Preview only; queued with the trade screens (after trade launch fixes). Bottom tabs: Work, Money, My business; installers also get "Add job".

Layout:
- Title: "Money".
- Big green card: "Landing in your account {next pay date}", the total, and "{N} jobs".
- Two white tiles: "This month" and "All time".
- "Jobs" list: owner surname + suburb, what was done (Safety check / Fixes / Install), the amount, and a status pill:
  - "Coming {day}" (blue)
  - "Being checked" (blush: waiting on our sign-off)
  - "Paid {date}" (mint)
- "Download statements ›" link.
- NO wording about payment terms — it's all in the agreement.

Data comes from the payouts records. Amounts are the trade's own pay, never the $349 customer price or margins. Payouts approximated as AP approval + 7 days until actual payout records exist; manual marking only, no money moves automatically.
