Jo-approved trade "My business" tab for electricians AND installers. Preview only; queued with the trade screens (after trade launch fixes). Bottom tabs: Work, Money, My business; installers also get "Add job".

Layout:
- Title: "My business" and the business name.
- "Taking new jobs" switch: off = "Paused, no new offers until you switch back on". Allocation MUST respect this switch — a paused business receives no offers.
- Pink alert card, only when a licence or insurance expires within 30 days: "{Doc} runs out in {N} days. Snap the new certificate so jobs keep coming", with an Upload button. On expiry: automatically stop offers AND add a Needs me item for the office.
- "Paperwork" list: electrical licence, public liability, "Your rates", Agreement — each with a status pill ("All good" / "{N} days" / "Set" / "Signed").
- "Where you work": "Up to {N} min from {base suburb}". Editable; it controls which offers they get. Google Maps drive time — until Maps is connected use the postcode proxy and say so.
- "The team": boss and workers with roles, and "+ Add someone" (reuses the existing worker invite).
- Footer links: Bank details · Your agreement · Sign out.
