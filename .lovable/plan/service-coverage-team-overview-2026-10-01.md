# Service Coverage team overview

## Goal
Make Service Coverage show, at a glance, where Home Insulation Victoria has usable REC and installer teams, who those partners are, and how many approved people are available.

## Build
- Extend the existing coverage read to include approved business members and approved partner workers, without changing operational records.
- Add a top-level partner capacity summary for REC businesses, installer businesses, approved workers, verified service areas, and setup gaps.
- Replace the basic partner cards with a clear team directory showing trade, people count, named team members, verified postcode count, declared base/radius, exclusions, and readiness.
- Add an area-by-area coverage matrix based on active enquiry locations, listing matching REC and installer businesses and counts so partnership gaps are immediately visible.
- Preserve the existing enquiry coverage table and link to partner management.

## Verification
- Run focused coverage tests or add pure-data tests where needed.
- Check current build diagnostics.
- Review the signed-in page at desktop and phone widths for readability and overflow.

## Boundaries
- Read-only presentation and coverage aggregation only.
- No partner approvals, assignments, invitations, customer messages, payments, workflow changes, or publishing.
