- Keep the newly simplified lifecycle pipeline and remove any remaining obsolete mockup references.
- Verify electrician and installer onboarding links, saving, private insurance files, agreement signing, office review, and mobile usability.
- Audit the whole homeowner-to-completion workflow and classify each area as ready, configuration-dependent, unverified, or missing.
- Review security findings, current errors, dependencies, and test/build health; fix safe launch-blocking issues within the existing rules.
- Provide the exact shareable onboarding URLs and explain the safest custom-domain/link setup without publishing unless you explicitly approve it.

## Important boundaries
- No customer communications, Xero/payment actions, external writes, Gate 1 rule changes, or publishing.
- Existing workflow and Activity 48 guards remain unchanged.
- Agreement wording remains marked as requiring legal review.
