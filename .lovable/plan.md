# Add job flow: three options, and when the eligibility check happens

## The answer, first

The eligibility check is never done by the installer, and never by Mitti. It is always the office's check, recorded on the job. When it happens depends on which Add job option the installer chose:

| Option | When the office checks eligibility |
|---|---|
| 1. I'm at the house | As soon as the Ready Check form is filled in and submitted — usually the same day. |
| 2. Ready Check later | After the booked visit, when the form comes in. |
| 3. Existing job (already measured/quoted) | When the office reviews what the installer uploaded — before any quote goes out. |

Until the office records the eligibility result, quoting is blocked by the portal. So no owner is ever quoted for a job that hasn't passed the check.

## The three Add job options (as you approved)

Every option asks the same customer details once — address, owner name, mobile, email, permission tick — and the electrician question. Nothing is asked twice.

1. **I'm at the house** — save the job and open the Ready Check form straight away (works instantly now that the installer's Mitti account is set up). The form answers (existing insulation, R-value, measured area, removal, access) are exactly what the eligibility check reads.
2. **Ready Check later** — owner details plus the booked appointment date; measurements optional. Office checks eligibility after the visit.
3. **Existing job** — measurements (area + how it was measured), the installer's quoted price and what it includes, the below-R2 confirmation, the fee-awareness tick, and uploads (measure-up, photos, job sheet). This option skips the Ready Check and goes straight to office review. If facts are missing, the office arranges only what's missing — the installer is never asked again.

## What the eligibility check looks at

The office check on a job needs: a final Ready Check submission (or supplied existing evidence under review), installer attendance with time and geotagged photo, ceiling-space photos, measured area and method, access assessment, existing insulation status with type/photos and depth or R-value, the removal answer, and the office's check of any previous Activity 48 work at that property. Items that are unclear or conflicting go to office review, never to a guessed result.

## Changes to make (small, presentation only)

1. **Existing job option tidied**: remove the "No — Do our Ready Check" branch inside it (options 1 and 2 already cover that). Adding an existing job goes straight to office review.
2. **Installer's job page**: after the Ready Check form is submitted or an existing job is added, show plainly: "Waiting on Home Insulation Victoria to review this job — you'll hear when it's ready to quote." Neutral wording; no eligibility details are shown to the installer.
3. **Office job page**: the existing eligibility panel stays the single place the check is recorded; no new screens or rules.
4. Nothing else changes — no locked rules, prices, stage names or emails are touched.

## Out of scope

No customer or installer emails, no Mitti/Xero calls, no database changes, no publishing, and no changes to eligibility or workflow rules. This is the Add job flow and its wording only.
