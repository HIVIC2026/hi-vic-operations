## Unsure – please confirm

1. I chose "Home Insulation Victoria" over "Us" for who is waiting. Owners and installers see it too, and "Us" would be unclear on their screens.
2. "Spot saved, needs an installer" and "Spot saved, photos sent" would now share the tag "Spot saved". That breaks "each tag unique", but keeps your rule that the same situation shows the same words. Is that OK?
3. The older stages (awaiting_electrician, assessment_*, ready_for_installer_quotes, awaiting_quote_acceptance) are kept with similar words so old jobs still read clearly. Should they say exactly the same as their newer stages instead? That would mean two stages share a tag.
4. "Photos missing", "Final check" and "Ready to lodge" replace "Evidence outstanding", "AP Review" and "Ready for VEEC". Is "VEECs" acceptable to staff as a term?
5. On owner screens, the last three install stages (photos missing, final check, ready to lodge) all show "Install done – we're finishing the paperwork". Is one shared message right, or do owners need to see the difference?
6. The owner wording for ready_check_awaiting_payment assumes these older jobs are now free Ready Checks, as decided on 19 Oct.
7. Wherever it says [date], the screen fills in the booked date. The words around it stay the same on every screen.
