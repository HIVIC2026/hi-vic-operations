# Activity 48 paperwork and homeowner forms

## Goal
Build one controlled Activity 48 document journey using current official ESC/VEU forms and wording, with each document appearing at the correct point and blocking progression when legally required.

## Proposed document timeline

1. **Before quote acceptance**
   - Show the current VEU consumer information, consumer rights and HI VIC commercial terms.
   - Keep eligibility and the free installer Ready Check separate from formal PIESA and compliance declarations.

2. **At quote acceptance**
   - Capture the commercial quote acceptance and payment terms.
   - Do not finalise the VEEC assignment timing until the latest official Activity 48 assignment form and guidance confirm whether a conditional pre-install signature is permitted.
   - If pre-install signing is permitted, retain a clearly conditional assignment that cannot become effective before installation. If not, move the assignment signature to completion without weakening the quote acceptance record.

3. **After an installation date is booked**
   - Present the official Regulation 23A owner information-sharing consent.
   - Capture owner signature and AP confirmation.
   - Submit or manually record the ESC installation notification in the official window: no more than 5 days and at least 24 hours before installation.

4. **After deposit, before installation**
   - Record the electrician-only PIESA using the current official form.
   - Enforce the current PIESA validity window and any rectification/Certificate of Electrical Safety requirement.
   - Confirm from the latest Activity 48 guide exactly who must receive a PIESA copy and when; then create the corresponding delivery records.
   - For life-support premises, require the exact official written consent or medical-plan evidence before PIESA/installation.

5. **Immediately before work starts**
   - Lead installer completes the official Installer Safety Assessment.
   - Confirm all required homeowner documents were provided before work: current consumer factsheet, conduct/dispute information, PIESA and any electrical certificate required by the official guide.
   - Block installation until all mandatory pre-install records and timing conditions pass.

6. **During and immediately after installation**
   - Capture the prescribed photographs, videos, floor plan, product/R-value/area records and installer declaration.
   - Record the customer co-payment and evidence of payment using the current official minimum and prescribed evidence.
   - Generate the completion pack: tax invoice, warranties, instructions, contacts, dispute information and every required signed copy.

7. **VEEC assignment and AP review**
   - Populate the official assignment form from verified final facts: consumer, premises, AP, installer and certification, installed products/areas, benefit and co-payment.
   - Enforce the legally confirmed signing parties and order; request another customer signature only where the official form requires it.
   - Give the homeowner the assignment copy at paper signing or within the confirmed electronic-delivery deadline.
   - Require AP review, complete evidence and an effective assignment before VEEC lodgement.

8. **VEEC lodgement and retention**
   - Record Registry submission reference, quantity, date, outcome and any corrections.
   - Retain every prescribed Activity 48 record for the latest confirmed statutory period; reconcile the portal’s current six/seven-year inconsistency before changing retention logic.

## Official-source work first
- Retrieve the latest Activity 48 Guide, General Accreditation Conditions, PIESA form, Owner Consent template, Installer Safety Assessment, VEEC Assignment form, current Specifications and Guidelines directly from ESC/DEECA.
- Record title, source URL, publication/effective date and version for every document.
- Compare July 2026 updates against the earlier April 2026 material.
- Produce a requirement matrix with: document, responsible party, fields, signatures, recipient, deadline, evidence and retention.
- Mark anything not stated by an official source as **Needs ESC/legal confirmation**; do not invent wording or rules.

## Portal implementation
- Add the verified official documents as immutable, versioned templates with their source metadata.
- Replace free-text office attestations with structured records where the official form defines fields or signatures.
- Keep uploaded originals and generated signed copies private in each Job File, with hashes, signer identity, timestamps and delivery evidence.
- Build a single per-job compliance checklist showing **not due**, **ready**, **waiting**, **overdue** and **complete**, plus the exact next action and responsible party.
- Regenerate documents when relevant facts change, preserve superseded versions, and require customer reconfirmation only for legally material changes.
- Keep customer communications manual and locked until explicitly enabled; no automatic sends, ESC submissions, payments, Xero actions or VEEC creation during this work.

## Corrections already identified for verification
- The portal currently describes a conditional VEEC assignment at quote acceptance. This must be checked against the latest official form before it is retained or moved.
- The portal currently records PIESA delivery to both owner and installer at least 24 hours before installation; the latest guide/form must confirm that exact obligation.
- The portal contains both six-year and seven-year retention references; use only the latest confirmed statutory period.
- The exact Activity 48 minimum co-payment must be sourced from the current Specifications rather than assumed from existing portal copy.
- The current Compliance Setup label “Installer PIISA” will be corrected when the official form set is implemented.

## Verification
- Test each role and every stage transition with complete, missing, expired, superseded and changed paperwork.
- Verify electronic signatures, immutable snapshots, copy delivery, PIESA expiry, the ESC notification window, co-payment evidence and VEEC blockers.
- Run the full automated suite and signed-in browser walkthroughs for homeowner, installer, REC and admin.
- Deliver a final plain-English checklist stating exactly what HI VIC must upload/configure, what is automated, and what remains a manual Registry action.

## Boundaries
- No legal wording will be drafted as if official.
- No requirement will be activated from an outdated or unverified document.
- This work does not publish the portal or enable live communications, payments, Xero, Mitti or ESC/VEEC submissions.
