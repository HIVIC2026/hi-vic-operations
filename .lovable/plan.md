# Check only: where the portal doesn't match the 26 Oct rules

Nothing has been changed. This is a list for Jo to choose from. Each fix would be a separate change, and only if Jo asks.

## Payments (Gate 4)
1. **PIESA fee amount.** Now: the fee can come from the quote line or a setting, so it could be something other than $349. Rule: always $349 inc GST. **Medium.**
2. **PIESA paid separately.** Now: everything is on one invoice, and paying enough on it unlocks the PIESA. Rule: the PIESA fee is paid separately after acceptance. **Medium.** Unsure: does "separately" mean its own invoice or just its own payment?
3. **Install booked before deposit.** Now: the install day is booked first, then the owner has 48 hours to pay the deposit, and they can get "Your install is booked". Rule: the install is only booked once the 10% deposit is paid. **Medium.**
4. **Wrong payment unlocks the install.** Now: the install booking uses the same check as the PIESA, so the PIESA fee alone can let it through. Rule: each booking needs its own step's payment. **Medium.**
5. **Old 5% deposit setting.** Now: a 5% rate for jobs of $20k or more is still in the settings, although the newer payment maths uses 10%. Rule: a flat 10%. **Small.** Unsure whether anything still uses the 5%.
6. **Old half-and-half balance.** Now: an old helper still splits the balance into two halves. Rule: no instalments, just the balance on install day. **Small to medium.** Unsure whether owners ever see it.
7. **"Deposit" means the PIESA fee in places.** Now: some older parts call the PIESA fee the "deposit". Rule: the PIESA fee and the 10% deposit are different steps. **Medium.**

Matches already: all eight payment statuses exist, and admin overrides need a reason and are logged. The fixes are paid in full before booking.

## Job stages
8. **The stage list is different.** Now: the portal uses an older, longer list of stages (for example "measured estimate", "paired PIESA and install window", "installer quotes"). Rule: exactly the 24 stages listed. **Big.**
9. **Missing stages.** Now: there are no separate stages for Spot offered, Spot saved, Office review, Awaiting PIESA fee, Awaiting fixes payment or Awaiting 10% deposit. Rule: each is its own stage. **Big** (part of 8).
10. **Old next-action wording.** Now: some jobs still show next actions like "Generate installer quotes". Rule: one installer quote, payment-led steps. **Medium.**
- Unsure: whether installer jobs from Mitti always start at "Ready Check done".

## Ready Check
11. **Owner signs before the visit.** Now: there is a "before any visit" paperwork set the owner signs (contact/privacy, sharing details with the trade, ownership, life support). Rule: the owner signs nothing before the Ready Check. **Medium.** Unsure whether it actually blocks every Ready Check. The quote paperwork itself is signed at quote acceptance, which matches.

## PIESA booking
12. **Owner can suggest their own days.** Now: the electrician offers 3 times, but the owner can also put forward their own days. Rule: the electrician offers 3 times and the owner picks one. **Medium.**
13. **Old jobs still use owner-first booking.** Now: older jobs carry on with the owner picking times first. Rule: the electrician offers times. **Medium.**
- Unsure: whether HIV choosing the electrician (automatically or by hand) works in every case.

## Quote
- No mismatch found: the quote comes from the one assigned installer. Unsure: whether a different installer could be assigned between the Ready Check and the quote.

## PIESA 30-day deadline
14. **Deadline after fixes.** Now: when fixes are needed, the 30 days still count from the first PIESA date. Rule: count from the Section C sign-off. **Medium.**

## Installer-sourced jobs
15. **The installer's choice.** Now: the installer picks an electrician (our electrician / my usual / a different one). Rule: the installer picks "HIV manages it" or "Installer manages it" for each job. **Big.**
16. **HIV-managed price.** Now: the PIESA part of the price can follow the electrician's rate. Rule: installer price + $349 PIESA + $349 AP fee − rebate. **Medium.**

## Texts, emails and wording
17. **Texts after 8pm.** Now: sign-in and signing codes are allowed after 8pm. Rule: no customer texts after 8pm. **Small.** (Jo to say whether codes the owner asks for count.)
18. **"Call us" in the quote email.** Now: the owner's quote email can say "Questions? Reply to this email or call us on ...". Rule: no phone number and no "call us". **Small.**
19. **PIESA name.** Now: owner and trade screens still say "Safety check" or "Electrical safety check". Rule: "Pre Insulation Electrical Safety Assessment (PIESA)". **Medium** (many screens).
20. **Eligibility words.** Now: "Likely eligible", "Check onsite", "Not eligible", plus "Needs review". Rule: "Likely Eligible", "Needs Onsite Confirmation", "Does Not Appear Eligible". **Small.**
