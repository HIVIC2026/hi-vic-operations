How a HIV job runs

For Lorraine, Dad and Tom. This page shows every step of a job, from the first enquiry to the VEECs being created and everyone being paid. For each step it says who does it, what the portal does by itself, and the exact moment the office has to do something. Correct as at 25 October 2026.

How to read this

Each row of the step table is one step. Read across: who acts, whether the portal does it or the office must, which form or VEU paperwork is filled in and who signs it, which emails and texts go out, what money moves, and what holds the job up. Anything in the Office colour needs a person in the office. Everything else happens by itself. When something gets stuck, it appears on the office Needs me list.

The two rules every trade must know

No complete Mitti form, no payment

We pay for the safety check, the fixes and the install only when that Mitti form is 100% filled in and the trade has pressed Complete. Every photo and video is taken in Mitti on site, so it is geotagged. Until then their pay shows as Held, with the reason. If anything is missing, the ESC won't accept the rebate claim.

Install within 30 days of the official safety check

The 30 days start on the date of the official PIESA. Electrical fixes never restart the clock. If the window runs out, a new safety check is needed and whoever's move it was pays for it.

The office only does these things

  1. Approve trades: check each business, its insurance and rates, each installer's insulation certificate and White Card, and each electrician business. Set people up in Mitti by hand while automatic Mitti invites are off.
  2. Choose the installer for each of our leads once the owner saves a spot. Choose again if they say "Not for me" or don't answer in 24 hours.
  3. Find an electrician only when nobody can take the safety check (offers go out by themselves).
  4. Check every quote and press Send. Sort out held quotes (the reason is shown).
  5. Sign our part of the ESC Owner's Consent form after the owner signs it.
  6. Lodge the ESC install notification in the VEU Registry, 24 hours to 5 days before the install, and type the reference into the job. Update or withdraw it if the day moves.
  7. Final check: review the finished job as the AP and press Approve.
  8. Create the VEECs in the VEU Registry and answer any Registry queries.
  9. Follow up stuck jobs from Needs me (calls, mostly): owner hasn't confirmed the safety check after 5 days, rebate form unsigned after 10 days, our rebate-form signature held for more than 24 hours, 10 days left on the 30-day clock, a lapsed window (and invoice the repeat safety check if it was the owner's move), a second "couldn't get in", an electrician who hasn't given fixes times or confirmed install-day attendance, a Ready Check still missing answers after 24 hours, a failed pre-start check, a payment overdue or not in Xero, a message that failed.
  10. Emails: match emails the portal can't place, and Apply or Ignore change requests the automatic reply can't handle (for example a cancel after the safety check is booked).
  11. Cancel or Hold a job when asked, with a reason. Refunds are recorded by hand.
  12. Check jobs flagged as a second request at the same address, and forms started straight in Mitti while automatic pickup is off.

Items 6 and 8 move to the Registry robot once Jo switches it on. Item 4 becomes "held quotes only" once automatic quote sending is on.

Switched off at launch, on when Jo flips it

  • Off All real emails and texts. The master switch "customer communications" is off, so nothing reaches a real owner or trade. Every text type also has its own switch, and new types start off. Test jobs always go to the test inbox and phone.
  • Off Automatic quote sending. Quotes are prepared by themselves and wait for the office to press Send. This one is set in the code, so turning it on needs a small code change, not just a Settings switch.
  • Off ESC Registry robot. Needs the Settings switch, a server setting, the Registry login, a "live" setting and a first watched run to check its field map. Until then the office does the install notification and VEEC creation by hand.
  • Off Card payments (Stripe). Need the live keys and the switch. Until then owners pay by bank transfer only.
  • Off Automatic installer offers. The office picks the installer. Staying off after launch.
  • Off Automatic Mitti invites for new trades (not yet tested against Mitti).
  • Off Reply drafts in Outlook.
  • Off Hold messages. Hold never sends anything.
  • Waiting Missing documents: the VEU consumer factsheet PDF, the ECOWOOL warranty and the Google review link. Each message goes without the missing piece, and ECOWOOL jobs hold our rebate-form signature.
  • On already (still waiting for the customer communications switch): automatic electrician offers; the electrical fixes quote sends itself; one automatic reply to a new person who emails info@ asking about insulation; the estimate follow-ups on days 2, 7 and 21 (Jo approved 25 Oct). Every other email and text wording in this guide is approved in the code.
  • To confirm in Settings: automatic pickup of forms started in Mitti, and the Xero payment check.

Questions staff get asked

Which insulation products can we install?
  • Bradford Gold R5, R6, R7 (70-year warranty)
  • Fletchers Pink Batts R5, R6, R7 (lifetime warranty)
  • Earthwool R5 (50-year warranty)
  • ECOWOOL R5 (warranty PDF not on file yet, so our rebate-form signature waits on ECOWOOL jobs)

The product must be on the ESC register, in date on the install day, and reach R5.0 or more.

Who can install?

An approved installer business. Each installer person needs a Certified Insulation Installer certificate checked by the office (the lead needs the Full level), a White Card checked by the office, the ESC privacy notice ticked by them personally, their signature on the job's safety plan, and a Mitti login (we pay for the seat).

Who can do the safety check and the fixes?

A licensed electrician from a Registered Electrical Contractor that is fully onboarded with us, with an agreed safety check rate. Anyone else holds the quote as "Electrician not onboarded" and gets one invite. Installer's own customers: the installer picks once at Add a job (their usual electrician or ours). Our leads: the installer's usual electrician if onboarded, otherwise one in the area. The offer goes out by itself.

How and when does the owner pay, and why bank transfer for the big amounts?
  • Safety check at acceptance: card or bank.
  • Fixes in full when approved: card or bank.
  • 10% of (quote total − safety check) to book the install: bank only. The day is held 48 hours.
  • Balance on install day: bank only. They can pay in full any time.

Card surcharges are banned, so we absorb the card fee. That's affordable on the two smaller amounts, and it means the safety check and fixes never wait for a transfer. Everything is paid against one Xero invoice, and the office never types payments in. Bank: Home Insulation Victoria, NAB, BSB 082-902, account 777966349.

The 30 days ran out. Who pays for the new safety check?

Whoever's move it was. The portal works out whose move and shows it on the job. If it was the owner's, they pay the same as their first check ($349 with our electrician, or their electrician's rate); the office invoices it, nothing is charged automatically. If it was the installer's, it comes out of their pay. Still with the lawyer: the owner-terms wording.

An owner wants to cancel, or we need to pause a job

Owners can cancel on their own link until the safety check is booked. After that it comes to the office, because money is involved. To cancel: open the job, More › Cancel, give a reason, and tick to email the owner and the installer. It stops every reminder and keeps the history. Hold pauses reminders and sends nothing. Refunds and cooling-off are still with the lawyer.

What is the Certificate of Electrical Safety?

The legal certificate an electrician issues for electrical work. When the safety check finds repairs, the electrician does them on a separate visit and uploads the certificate in the Mitti A4 form. The portal files it, updates the official PIESA, and sends the owner a copy in the completion pack.

What warranties does the owner get, and who do they call?

The manufacturer's product warranty (5 years or more) comes with the "All done" email and again in the completion pack. The installer gives a 1-year workmanship warranty. If something is wrong after the install, the owner calls the installer first, then us, then the ESC.
