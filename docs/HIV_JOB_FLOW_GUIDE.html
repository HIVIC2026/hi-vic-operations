01

The two rules every trade must know

Rule 1

No complete Mitti form, no payment

  • We pay for the safety check, the fixes and the install only when that Mitti form is 100% finished and the trade has pressed Complete.
  • Every photo and video is taken in Mitti, on site, so it is geotagged. Camera-roll photos don't count.
  • If anything is missing, the ESC won't accept the rebate claim. The pay shows as Held with the reason until it's fixed.
  • On install day, the installer doesn't leave site until the portal texts "All filled in".

Rule 2

Install within 30 days of the safety check

  • The 30 days start on the date of the official electrical safety check (the PIESA).
  • Electrical fixes don't restart the clock. They must be done inside the same 30 days.
  • Install days can only be offered inside the window.
  • If it runs out, a new safety check is needed. Whoever's move it was pays for it.

Say both rules to every installer and electrician when they start, and again whenever a form comes back incomplete.

Questions staff get asked

Tap a question to open it. Anything marked Still open is Jo's to decide: don't guess, ask Jo.

Which insulation products can we install?
  • Bradford Gold: R5, R6, R7
  • Fletchers Pink Batts: R5, R6, R7
  • Earthwool: R5
  • ECOWOOL: R5

The product must be on the ESC register, in date on the install day, and add up to at least R5.0. The Earthwool R5 register entry expires on 23 Feb 2027.

Warranties sent to owners: Pink Batts lifetime, Earthwool 50 years, Bradford Gold 70 years. The ECOWOOL warranty PDF isn't on file yet, so on ECOWOOL jobs our signature on the rebate form waits until it arrives.

Who can install?

The installer business applies online and the office approves it: ABN, $5 million public liability, its installers, its electrician, its area, its rates, bank details and the signed agreement. To get our leads it must do free Ready Checks.

Each installer person needs:

  • A Certified Insulation Installer certificate, checked by the office. The lead must hold the Full level.
  • A White Card, uploaded and checked by the office.
  • The ESC privacy notice, ticked by them personally.
  • Their signature on the job's safety plan before work starts.
  • A Mitti login. The portal sets it up when the office approves them, and we pay for the seat.
Who can do the safety check and the fixes?

A licensed electrician from a Registered Electrical Contractor who is fully onboarded with us: approved, with an agreed safety check rate. Anyone else holds the quote as "Electrician not onboarded" and gets one invite.

  • Installer's own customers: the installer picks once at Add a job: their usual electrician or ours.
  • Our leads: the installer's usual electrician if onboarded, otherwise one in the area.

The offer goes out automatically. The electrician offers the owner 3 times, and the owner picks one or suggests days.

Fixes: the electrician ticks what they'll do and gives one total price. With our electrician we add $100. With the installer's own electrician the owner pays their price exactly.

What insurance do trades need?
  • Installer business and electrician: public liability of at least $5 million, with the certificate uploaded. If it lapses, no new jobs.
  • Electricians: a current licence. Warnings at 60 and 30 days before expiry.
  • Workers comp: "where required by law". The portal doesn't require a document.

Still open: a yearly public liability re-check reminder.

What does the owner get from us, and when?
  1. Quote acceptance and Customer Agreement, with the receipt and the VEU consumer factsheet in the "You're in" email.
  2. Official PIESA, which they confirm on their link. A copy at least 24 hours before the install.
  3. ESC Owner's Consent Form, signed when they book the install day. A signed copy follows.
  4. Product warranty, with the "All done" email.
  5. Final invoice and the VEEC Assignment Form to sign, with a signed copy.
  6. Completion pack once paid in full: warranty, Certificate of Electrical Safety (if there were fixes) and our certificate of completion.
Why is the deposit and balance bank transfer only?
  • Card surcharges are banned, so we absorb the card fee. That's affordable on the safety check and the fixes, not on large amounts.
  • Card on the safety check and fixes means nothing waits for a bank transfer.
  • Bank transfer is free and goes against the one invoice number.
When does the 30-day window start?

On the date of the official safety check. Fixes never restart it. The owner ticks that they understand it when they pick a safety check time. Owners and installers get reminders at 20 and 10 days left, and the office gets one alert at 10 days left.

The 30 days ran out. Who pays for the new safety check?

Whoever's move it was. If it's the owner, they pay the same as the first check ($349 with our electrician, or their electrician's rate). If it's the installer, it comes out of their pay. The portal works out whose move it was and shows it to the office. Nothing is charged automatically yet.

Still open: the lawyer is wording this for the owner terms.

How do the VEECs get signed over?

After the install, the VEEC Assignment Form is signed in order: the lead installer, then us (automatically, once the PIESA copies, ESC notice, warranty copy, privacy notices and consent are all recorded), then the owner on their link. The warranty now goes with the "All done" email, so our signature no longer waits for full payment. The office is told only if our signature is held for more than 24 hours.

What does the owner pay in total?

The quote shows the insulation (and removal if needed), the safety check and our $349 AP fee, then takes off the VEU rebate. The owner doesn't claim the rebate; it comes off straight away. Fixes are extra, only if the safety check finds something. On our leads, difficult access adds a line only when the installer has an agreed difficult-access fee.

What if the crew can't get in?
  • Ready Check: the installer taps "Couldn't get in?" with a reason, and the visit is rebooked.
  • Install day: the owner is told straight away and the installer offers new days. Nobody is charged. The 30-day clock keeps running. The office is told only on a second no-access.
An owner wants to change a day or cancel
  • If they email us, they get an automatic reply with their own link to change the day.
  • The owner can cancel on their own link until the safety check is booked. After that, cancelling goes through the office, because money is involved.
  • To cancel: open the job, More › Cancel, give a reason. Tick the boxes to email the owner and the installer. It stops every reminder and keeps the history.
  • Hold pauses reminders and sends no messages.

Still open: refunds and cooling-off (lawyer).

When do installers and electricians get paid?
  • Our leads: 7 days after AP approval, whether or not the owner has paid.
  • Installer's own customers: when the owner pays the balance.
  • Always only when the Mitti form is 100% complete. Until then the Money page shows Held with the reason.
Who does the owner call if something's wrong after the install?

The installer first, then us, then the ESC. Workmanship is covered by the installer for 12 months. The product is covered by the manufacturer's warranty.
