Two ways a job comes in

From the moment the owner accepts the quote, both kinds of job follow the same steps. Before that they differ.

A1 · The installer's own customer
  • The installer adds the job in the portal: owner's name, address, mobile, email and a tick that the owner said we can contact them.
  • The installer picks the electrician once, at Add a job: their usual electrician or ours.
  • The installer arranges the Ready Check visit with the owner directly.
  • The installer's business owner enters the price. Their staff never see prices.
  • We don't mark up the installer's price. We add our AP fee and the electrician's safety check.
  • The installer is paid when the owner pays the final balance.
A2 · Our lead
  • The owner fills in the calculator on our website and gets an estimate.
  • The owner taps Save my spot. The office picks one installer.
  • The installer offers the owner 3 visit times.
  • The price comes from our price list (R5, R6, R7 per m², removal per m²).
  • The installer and electrician must not discuss price with the owner. We send all prices.
  • The installer is paid 7 days after AP approval.

Step by step

Step 1 · our leads only

Enquiry and estimate

OwnerAutomatic

What happens

  • The owner uses the calculator on our website.
  • The portal works out a first eligibility answer from what they told us.
  • Missing suburbs and postcodes are filled in automatically.
  • Office does nothing here. Enquiries is one list you can search.

Automatic

  • Estimate and Save my spot email, plus a reminder text.
  • If they don't save a spot: reminders on day 2, 7 and 21, then the enquiry closes a week later.
  • Not eligible, or not in our area: a polite email.

Form

  • Website calculator, then the owner's estimate page.

VEU rules covered

  • No cold calling: the owner contacts us first.
  • One ceiling upgrade per home: "previous upgrade" question, office reviews.
  • Life support asked up front.
Step 2

The job starts

OwnerInstallerOfficeAutomatic

What happens

  • Our lead: the owner saves their spot. The job shows on Needs me. For launch the office picks one installer. Automatic installer offers are switched off on purpose and can be turned on later.
  • The installer has 24 hours. "Not for me" or no answer comes back to the office to choose again.
  • Installer's customer: the installer uses Add a job. The job starts straight away.

Automatic

  • Spot saved email and text to the owner.
  • Offer text, email and app alert to the installer. Texts wait overnight, 8pm to 7am.
  • The Mitti Ready Check form is created for the job.

VEU rules covered

  • Permission to contact: the tick at Add a job.
  • Only one open job per address. A repeat at the same address is linked and the office is told.
Step 3

Ready Check visit

InstallerOwnerAutomatic

What happens

  • Our lead: the installer offers 3 times on 3 days. The owner taps one or suggests others.
  • The installer measures each ceiling area, films it and photographs any existing insulation with a ruler.
  • They rate the ceiling access, note every issue with a photo, and pick the product.
  • No safe access: the form stops and the office is told.
  • On our leads the installer ticks "I will not discuss pricing".

Automatic

  • Booked email and text, and a day-before text.
  • 6:30am text to the installer with the day's jobs and Mitti links.
  • The portal draws the ceiling plan from the measurements.
  • "Nearly there" text if answers are missing.

Form

  • Mitti A1 Ready Check (installer's customer) or A2 Ready Check (our lead).

Money

  • None. The Ready Check is free.

VEU rules covered

  • Geotagged before video of every area.
  • Existing insulation assessed, depth photo with a ruler.
  • Floor plan with each area's size.
Step 4

Quote

InstallerOfficeAutomatic

What happens

  • The installer presses Complete in Mitti.
  • Installer's customer: the business owner gets one text with a link to enter their price and inclusions.
  • For launch: the office opens Needs me › Quotes to check, checks it, and presses Send. Jo has switched automatic sending off on purpose. Once she turns it on, a quote that passes every check goes to the owner by itself.
  • A quote is held, with the reason shown, if: the price isn't in, the electrician isn't onboarded, or another check fails.

Automatic

  • The quote is prepared as soon as the form is complete.
  • After Send: quote email and text to the owner.
  • Reminders 3 days after sending, and 2 days before the price expires.
  • If the owner says "Not for me", the installer and office are told.

Screen

  • Office: Needs me › Quotes to check.
  • Installer business owner: "Your price" card on the job page.
Step 5

Owner accepts and pays the safety check

OwnerAutomatic

What happens

  • On their secure link the owner reads the quote, ticks to accept it and the Customer Agreement, ticks they're 18+ and the owner, and types their name.
  • They tap "Sign and pay".
  • When they book the safety check, they tick that they understand the install must happen within 30 days of it, or a new check is needed. They can't send their times until it's ticked. BUILT, NOT LIVE

Automatic

  • The Xero invoice is created.
  • "You're in!" email with a receipt. The installer is told.
  • The safety check offer goes to the electrician automatically.

Money

  • The safety check: $349 with our electrician, or the approved electrician's agreed rate. Card or bank transfer.

VEU rules covered

  • The AP contracts directly with the owner.
  • Minimum $200 owner co-payment is met by this payment.
Step 6

Electrical safety check (PIESA)

ElectricianOwnerAutomatic

What happens

  • The electrician has 24 hours to offer the owner 3 times. The owner picks one.
  • On site the electrician photographs themselves holding their licence, and the switchboard cables.
  • They check life support, mains power, solar and batteries, ceiling wiring, downlights and every appliance or flue.
  • If repairs are needed they give one total price and tick what's included, with how many downlights and safety switches. "Other" needs a description. BUILT, NOT LIVE
  • On our leads they tick that they won't discuss any prices with the owner. BUILT, NOT LIVE
  • The owner then confirms the check on their secure link: one tick and their typed name. No code.

Automatic

  • Booking emails and texts, and a day-before text.
  • The official PIESA form is filled in from the Mitti answers.
  • The electrician gets the official PIESA on their job page and by email, and again after any fixes. BEING BUILT
  • "Please confirm your safety check" email and text, reminders on days 1 and 3, office alert on day 5.
  • The install can't be booked until the owner confirms.
  • No repairs needed: the 30-day clock starts and the installer is told to offer install days.

Forms

  • Mitti A4 PIESA (electrician).
  • Owner's confirmation page on the secure link.
  • Official PIESA (filled in by the portal).

VEU rules covered

  • Safety check by a licensed electrician from an approved REC.
  • Owner's declaration on the PIESA.
  • Life support: written consent or medical plan, or no install.
  • Only IC or IC-4 rated downlights may be covered.
Step 6b · only when repairs are needed

Electrical fixes

ElectricianOwnerOfficeAutomatic

What happens

  • The fixes quote is the electrician's price. On our leads we add $100. On installer customers we add nothing.
  • When the electrician marks the safety check complete in Mitti, they get a text with a link to pick 3 to 5 times for the fixes. BEING BUILT
  • The quote waits for those times. Then the office checks it and presses Send.
  • The owner approves, pays in full and picks one of the electrician's times, all in one go.
  • Repairs happen before the install. The one exception: if the electrician ticked "Be on site at the start of the install day", they attend the install instead, and the office tells them the day.

Automatic

  • Booking the fixes visit creates the Mitti fixes form.
  • When it's complete, the Certificate of Electrical Safety is filed and the official PIESA is updated.
  • The 30 days still count from the safety check (PIESA) date, not the repair date. Fixes must be done well inside that window.
  • Anything missing makes one office item.

Form

  • Mitti A5 Electrical fixes: created, filled in and sent to the electrician's Mitti automatically when the fixes visit is booked. It records the repairs done, the certificate number, date and copy, a geotagged photo of the finished work, and their signature.
  • The electrician issues the Certificate of Electrical Safety themselves through Energy Safe Victoria. We only collect it.

Money

  • The fixes, paid in full by card or bank transfer before the visit.

VEU rules covered

  • Hazards fixed before the install.
  • Certificate of Electrical Safety for the electrical work.
Step 7

Book the install day

InstallerOwnerAutomatic

What happens

  • The installer offers 3 days, each with an arrival time, inside the 30 days.
  • The owner taps a day, signs the ESC Owner's Consent Form and pays the deposit.
  • Or the owner suggests 1 to 5 days and they go back and forth. The office isn't involved.

Automatic

  • "Your install is booked" email, and a copy of the signed consent.
  • A day the owner suggested is held 48 hours, then released if the deposit isn't paid.
  • Reminders at 20 and 10 days left to whoever needs to act.
  • One office alert at 10 days left with nothing booked.
  • If the 30 days run out, whoever caused the delay pays for the new safety check.

Form

  • ESC Owner's Consent Form on the owner's secure link. We sign our part.

Money

  • Deposit: 10% of (quote total − safety check). Bank transfer only.

VEU rules covered

  • Owner's written consent at least 24 hours before, signed again if the date changes.
  • Install within 30 days of the safety check.
Step 8 · 24 hours to 5 days before

Before the install

OfficeInstallerAutomatic

What happens

  • ESC install notification: the Registry robot logs into the VEU Registry, fills in the notification from the job, submits it and saves the reference and a screenshot to the job. BEING SET UP
  • Until the robot is live, or if it fails, the office submits it by hand from the prepared details. A failed robot run shows on Needs me.
  • Installer: the lead opens the safety plan on the job page and adds a site note. Every crew member signs it with one tap.

Automatic

  • The PIESA copy goes to the owner and the installer at least 24 hours before.
  • Install-tomorrow texts to the installer and owner.
  • Pre-start check at noon the day before: the lead installer's licence is current, every crew member has read the privacy notice, the safety plan is signed. If it passes, the Mitti install day form is created.
  • The job won't start unless consent, notification and PIESA copies are all done at least 24 hours before, inside the 30 days.

Forms

  • ESC install notification (VEU Registry).
  • Safety plan (SWMS) on the portal job page.

VEU rules covered

  • Install notification 24 hours to 5 days before.
  • PIESA copy to owner and installer.
  • Every installer is a certified insulation installer.
  • Every installer has the ESC privacy notice.
  • Safe work method statement signed.
Step 9

Install day

InstallerAutomatic

What happens

  • Each installer is photographed at the front door with photo ID.
  • Solar and batteries checked, power isolated, locked out and tagged, switchboard photographed.
  • Product packaging photographed, insulation installed.
  • After video and depth photo with a ruler for every area.
  • They record who turned the power back on, and the lead signs.
  • Can't get in: the owner is told and the installer offers new days. Nobody is charged.

Automatic

  • The crew names are filled in from the booking.
  • The official PIISA is filled in from the answers.
  • Marked Complete and every photo is there: the install is recorded as done.

Forms

  • Mitti A3 Install day.
  • Official PIISA (filled in by the portal).

VEU rules covered

  • PIISA by the lead installer on the day, rechecked daily on longer jobs.
  • Power isolated and locked out.
  • Geotagged, timestamped photos and videos.
  • Approved product, R5.0 or more.
Step 10

After the install

OwnerInstallerAutomatic

What happens

  • The VEEC assignment form is signed in this order:
  • 1. The lead installer, from their job page.
  • 2. Us as the AP, signed automatically once the PIESA copies, notification, warranty copy, privacy notices and consent are all recorded.
  • 3. The owner, on their secure link.

Automatic

  • "All done" email and text with the "sign your rebate form" box.
  • Final invoice with the balance due.
  • Owner signing reminders on days 1, 3 and 7, then an office alert on day 10.
  • Signed copy emailed within 10 business days.
  • Once paid in full: completion pack with the product warranty attached.
  • Review request a week later.

Form

  • VEEC Assignment Form v1.1 (official ESC form).

Money

  • The balance, due on install day. Bank transfer only.

VEU rules covered

  • Assignment form signed after the install, owner gets a copy.
  • Product warranty of 5+ years given to the owner.
  • Invoice shows the AP, product, R-value, m², price and incentive.
Step 11

Final check and VEECs

Office

What happens

  • The office reviews the job as the AP.
  • It can't move to Ready for VEEC until every photo and video is in, the owner has signed, and the product is approved, in date and R5.0 or more.
  • The Registry robot creates the VEECs in the VEU Registry, using the install notification number, once all three people have signed the assignment form. BEING SET UP Until then, or if it fails, the office does it by hand.
  • Our-lead installers are paid 7 days after AP approval.

VEU rules covered

  • Product on the ESC register on the install date.
  • Records kept for at least 6 years. We keep signed copies for 7.
