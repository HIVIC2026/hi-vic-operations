About your electrical safety check

Victorian Government rules require a licensed electrician to check your ceiling is safe before any insulation goes in. It's called a Pre-Insulation Electrical Safety Assessment (PIESA) and costs $349.00.

What the electrician checks

  • Your switchboard and main switch, so power to the ceiling can be safely turned off, and that safety switches (RCDs) protect the ceiling circuits
  • The cables, connections and fittings in your ceiling, so they're safe to work around and cover with insulation
  • Downlights and other recessed lights, to see whether they're rated to be covered with insulation
  • Exhaust fans, flues and appliances in the ceiling space
  • Solar, battery or generator cables, if you have them

If something needs fixing

Common fixes are old downlights that can't be covered (replaced with IC-rated LED lights), missing safety switches, or old or damaged wiring. If any repairs are needed, we send you a separate quote for them first. Repairs are charged separately to this quote, and nothing is done without your OK. Once they're done, the electrician gives you a Certificate of Electrical Safety and your installation goes ahead. If a repair isn't possible, we'll explain your options before anything goes ahead.

After the check, please don't let anyone go into your ceiling before the installation – it can cancel the safety check.
