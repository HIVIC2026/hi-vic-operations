## Summary

The new firm-quote emails read well. They have no "spot" wording, no "about" prices and no blank gaps, the plain-text versions match the HTML, and the links go to the right page. Jobs created before you flip the switch stay on the old Save my spot flow. Pressing Proceed twice is safe.

**Fix these 2 before Monday:**

1. **Office Send buttons still send the old Save my spot email.** If the office presses Send on a new photo enquiry (Hot leads, Enquiries "send", a held email, or a reviewed-insulation send), the owner gets the old "Save your spot" email. That can be on top of the new preliminary estimate email, so they get two. If the office sends first, the new email never goes at all. I proved this with a test.
2. **Automatic replies to owner emails still talk about spots.** If a firm-quote owner emails "what happens next?" or "is my spot saved?", they get an automatic reply. Before Proceed it says "Answer 3 questions and press Save my spot" with a Save my spot link. After Proceed it says "your spot is saved… an installer will call to book your free Ready Check". Proven.

**Fix soon (ideally before launch):**

- If the Proceed page can't load for a moment (bad signal or a database hiccup), it says "This one's already been taken care of". The owner thinks they're done, but nothing was saved and no quote follows.
- If the website photos fail to copy at the start, the owner gets the old Save my spot email. Once the photos copy later, the job switches to the new flow and the spot reminders stop, so the owner is stuck between the two flows.
- The day-4 "on its way" email only looks at the first 500 firm-quote requests ever. After that, new owners never get it.
- Treat the switch as one-way. Turning it off, or moving its date earlier, moves owners between the old and new flows mid-way.
- The new emails aren't on the Templates page yet, so you can't preview them there.

Most of the 87 failing message tests are out of date (old wording or old test setups), not real bugs.

  - held in quiet hours;
  - sent once per job (key plus event);
  - not sent after the quote went out or the job closed.

- **Spot messages:** these senders skip firm jobs: spot saved, reminders, day 2/7/21, release, quick taps, area batch, campaign and recovery.

  - no "about" with prices, and no "You pay about";
  - no "spot" in firm messages;
