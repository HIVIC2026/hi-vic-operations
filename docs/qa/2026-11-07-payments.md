# Payments QA – HIV ops portal (checked Sat 7 Nov 2026, main at f49eda3)

## Plain-English summary

**All 4 problems from Thursday's review are still there. None are fixed.**

- **(a) Still there.** If the system hiccups just after it saves a card payment, the job can stay stuck. The owner has paid, but the job does not move on, the "You're in" email does not go, and the paid amount on the invoice may not update. When Stripe tries again, the system says "already have it" and skips the unfinished work. Bank transfers coming in from Xero have the same weak spot.
- **(b) Still there.** If a refund arrives before its payment has been saved, it is thrown away. If the database is slow at the moment a card payment comes in, the payment is also thrown away, but Stripe is told "all good", so it never sends it again. The owner has paid and the portal never finds out.
- **(c) Still there.** If the database is slow just before the "Spot saved" email is sent, the screen can say "Already sent to this owner" when nothing was sent.
- **(d) Still there.** The automatic Mitti form sweep only looks at the oldest 500 booked website jobs. Job 501 and later are never picked up.

**New problems found:**

- **Card money can be counted twice.** This happens when the same card payment is also entered in Xero more than 10 days later, or entered for a different amount (for example after Stripe's fee comes off), and the Stripe number is not in the Xero reference. I proved one case: an owner who still owed $1,325.50 showed as **fully paid**. That would release an own-customer installer's pay early and stop the balance reminders.
- **The deposit clean-up can wrongly cancel a paid install day.** It checks who has paid first, then cancels days one by one. A deposit that arrives in between is missed, so the day is released and the owner is told it wasn't paid.
- Smaller issues are in the table below.

**What checked out fine:** the 50% sums always add up to the right total to the cent, including $349 and $275 safety checks. Booking the install is blocked until the deposit is paid. One card payment can't be saved twice. TEST and live Stripe are kept apart. The "same amount, no Stripe number counts once" rule works inside 10 days.

**Before Monday, also check by hand** (I couldn't see the live settings): the payment rule switch is ON for real jobs (`payment_option_gate_enabled`), `STRIPE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true`, the live Stripe key is `sk_live_`, and both webhook secrets are set. If the switch is off, every real job stays locked at the safety check.
