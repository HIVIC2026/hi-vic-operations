# HIV portal — do the job rules actually hold? (checked 7 Nov 2026, code as of main f49eda3)

## Plain-English summary

Most of your rules are properly locked in by the system, not just hidden buttons. The safety check can't be booked until the $349 is in. The install can't be booked before the 50% deposit or after the 30-day date. Firm quotes always wait for you to press Send. A quote more than 15% off the estimate is held. Installers can't open each other's jobs. A missing or expired electrician licence stops approval. Owners who've paid can't cancel from My job. The TEST office login is fenced off to TEST jobs and TEST installers.

Five things need fixing or a decision before Monday:

1. **Bank detail changes aren't checked.** When an installer changes their bank account, it replaces the old one straight away. Nothing asks the office to OK it and nobody is told. That's a fraud risk.
2. **Installers can see the owner's price on your leads by going around the portal.** The screens hide it, but the job history behind them includes lines like "Invoice raised … $4,321 inc GST" and "quote accepted (total $4,321)". An installer who's logged in can read those directly. I proved this on a practice copy of the database.
3. **A Lead Installer who isn't ESC-registered can still be picked** in the normal crew picker. It's only caught later, at the GO / DON'T START check. The in-house booking screen does block it properly.
4. **The quote counts as "accepted" when the owner taps Accept, before the $349 is paid.** The safety check still can't be booked without the money, so nothing is unsafe. But "accepted" doesn't mean "paid".
5. **DON'T START is a warning, not a lock.** Missing Part C or certificate stops the GO and stops the installer's pay. It doesn't stop the install being recorded as done.

Also worth knowing:
- Email "unsubscribe" never cancels a job.
- STOP only cancels jobs still at the enquiry or saved-spot stage. Later jobs just get "Do not contact". That matches your docs, but not the rule as you listed it.
- If you switch the firm-quote flow off, owners who already asked for a firm quote go back to getting Save-my-spot reminders.
