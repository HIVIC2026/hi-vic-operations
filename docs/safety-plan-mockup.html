High-risk work on this job

  • Risk of a person falling more than 2 metres (ladder, manhole, roof, ceiling joists)
  • Work on or near energised electrical installations or services (ceiling cables, switchboard, solar/battery)
  • Work that may disturb asbestos (home built before 1990, or asbestos suspected)

Hazards and the controls you use

  • Live electrical: power isolated, locked out and tagged before entry (solar, battery and generator too, following their instructions); PIESA read and its conditions followed; electrician on site when the PIESA requires; test for dead before touching cables.
  • Hazardous substances: if asbestos is suspected, do not disturb it — stop work; it is a licensed asbestos removalist's job before work continues; no chemicals or solvents used in the ceiling.
  • Biological (mould, animal waste): do not disturb droppings or mould dry; P2 mask and gloves; wash hands before eating.
  • Heat: work early, water and a break every 30 minutes, stop in extreme heat.
  • Noise and vibration: hearing protection for blowers and power tools.
  • Dust and irritants: P2 mask, goggles, gloves and long sleeves in the ceiling; removed material bagged in the ceiling.
  • Enclosed spaces: exits kept clear, lighting set up first, stay in contact with someone outside the ceiling.
  • Height (inside and outside the ceiling space): walk only on joists or crawl boards, never on plasterboard; ladder secured and footed with three points of contact; no roof access in wind or rain.
  • Fire and ignition sources: AS 3999 clearances kept to flues, chimneys, gas heaters, heat lamps, exhaust fans and downlights; cables over 400 mm in insulation as the PIESA directs.

How these are put in place: the lead installer directly supervises the crew, checks every control before anyone enters the ceiling and again at the start of each day, and stops work if any control cannot be followed.
