# Paid tester brief — Home Insulation Victoria portal (post on Airtasker / Upwork)

**Title:** Test a home-services booking website on your phone (about 3 hours, $150)

**What we need:** someone who is NOT a developer to use our new website on their own phone, like a real customer and tradie would, and tell us everything that is confusing, slow, broken or unclear. You will use TEST accounts and a TEST job only — nothing is real, nobody is charged, no real customer is contacted.

**You need:** an iPhone or Android phone, a laptop for writing notes, 3 hours in one sitting, good written English. Screen recordings (built into your phone) are a bonus.

**You'll get:** three TEST logins (owner, installer, electrician) and a TEST job link from us by email.

**Deliverable:** one spreadsheet, one row per problem: step number · what you did · what you expected · what happened · screenshot or recording · how bad (stops me / annoying / small). Plus a 5-line summary: the three worst problems and anything you liked.

---

## Test script (send to the tester with the logins)

Time each step roughly. If anything takes more than 10 seconds to load, write it down with the time.

**Part A — the home owner (phone)**
1. Open the website. Put in a Melbourne address, pick a ceiling size, pick "Some there". Do you understand your price and discount?
2. Register your interest with the TEST name/mobile/email we gave you.
3. Open the link in the email we send you. Answer the 3 quick questions and press Save my free spot.
4. Open the "My job" link. Can you tell what happens next and when?
5. Pick a Ready Check time when offered. Change it once. Then cancel and re-book.
6. When the quote arrives, read it. Is anything unclear? Accept it and sign. Pay the $349 with the TEST card we give you.
7. Confirm the safety check, approve the electrical fixes, pick an install day, and look at what you'd pay and when.

**Part B — the installer (phone, installer login)**
8. Log in. Find the TEST job. Offer three install days.
9. Open the safety plan and sign it.
10. Find what you'll be paid and when.

**Part C — the electrician (phone, electrician login)**
11. Log in. Accept the safety-check offer and offer three times.
12. Find the job's address, the owner's contact details, and your pay.

**Part D — general**
13. Rotate the phone, make the text bigger in phone settings, and repeat any two screens. Does anything break?
14. Anything you'd expect to be able to do but couldn't?
