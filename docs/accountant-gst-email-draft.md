# Email to our accountant — GST questions (draft for Jo to send)

**Subject:** GST check on how we invoice VEU ceiling insulation jobs

Hi [name],

Before we launch our new system I'd like you to confirm the GST treatment of how we bill a ceiling insulation job under the Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) program. We are an Accredited Person (AP A003654).

How a job works:
- We quote the owner a full price, then take off the VEU discount (we create the VEECs and sell them, so the discount is our money, not a government payment to the owner).
- The owner pays: $349 up front for the electrical safety check (non-refundable once done), any electrical fixes in full when they approve them, a 10% deposit to book the install, and the balance on install day. All on one Xero invoice.
- The electrician and the installer are separate businesses. Sometimes we pass the electrician's own fee straight through to the owner at cost; other times we charge our standard $349 and pay the electrician their own rate. We add $100 to electrical fixes when it's our electrician.

My questions:
1. **VEU discount:** is GST worked out on the price after the VEU discount (what the owner actually pays), and is our sale of the VEECs a separate taxable supply?
2. **Electrician pass-through:** when we pass the electrician's fee through at cost on our invoice, are we the principal (GST on the full amount, claim the electrician's GST as a credit) or should it be shown differently?
3. **The $349 up-front payment:** is GST payable when we receive it, given it is non-refundable once the check is done?
4. **Paying installers and electricians:** we plan to pay them from Xero bills. Should we use recipient-created tax invoices (RCTIs), or should they invoice us?
5. Anything else in this set-up that you'd change before we start?

Thanks,
