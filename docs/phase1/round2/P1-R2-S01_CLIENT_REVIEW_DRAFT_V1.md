## What this email is for

When someone completes the online enquiry and **tells us their ceiling already has some insulation**, we can't confirm eligibility from the website alone — we need to see the existing insulation. Everything else on their enquiry has passed our checks.

This email politely asks them to do **one easy, optional thing**: send a short phone video (or photos) of the existing ceiling insulation, so our office can review their enquiry sooner. It also gives them the online calculator so they can see their potential rebate.

It does **not** promise eligibility, a rebate amount, a price, or an installation date. Those are only confirmed later at the free onsite check.

## Why it's written this way

- **No promises we can't keep.** It says "potential" rebate and "if you can't do it safely, we'll check at the free onsite visit." No rebate figure, price, eligibility confirmation or install date is stated.
- **Safety first.** It explicitly tells people **not** to climb into or walk through the roof space, and frames the video as fully optional.
- **One clear next action.** Two simple buttons: *Open the calculator* and *Record or upload a video*.
- **Help / fallback.** If they can't record safely, they do nothing and we handle it at the onsite visit.
- **Consistent look.** Uses the same Home Insulation Victoria email design as the approved estimate emails.
