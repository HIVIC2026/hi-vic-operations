# From the Ready Check visit to the quote (our leads)

The installer measures the ceiling in Mitti. The portal turns that into the owner's quote. The office checks the quote and presses Send.

> **Built to be automatic, on hold for launch.** The portal can send the quote to the owner by itself as soon as every check passes. **For launch we've switched this off, so the office checks each quote and presses Send** (step 4). When we switch it on, only quotes the portal holds back will need you.

**1. On the day** *(automatic)*
- At 6:30am the installer gets a "jobs today" text with a link to that job's Mitti form (A2 Ready Check).

**2. The installer fills in the Ready Check in Mitti**
- For each ceiling area: length and width, a slow 360° video, and a ruler photo of any existing insulation.
- If there's existing insulation: whether it's being removed or topped up.
- How hard the access is, and a photo of each problem.
- They press **Complete**.

**3. The portal checks it** *(automatic)*
- It draws the ceiling diagram for the ESC from the measurements.
- If answers are missing, the installer gets a "Nearly there" text listing what's missing.
- If it's still missing after 24 hours, it shows on Needs me as **Chase installer**.

**4. The quote is prepared** *(automatic)*, **then you check it and press Send** *(office, for now)*
- The portal prices it from our price list:
  - insulation per m² (R5 $30, R6 $32, R7 $35)
  - removal of old insulation per m², if needed
  - the electrical safety check (PIESA): always $349 on our leads, whoever does it
  - our AP fee
  - minus the VEU rebate
  - a "difficult access" fee only if the installer rated it difficult and has an agreed fee
- It shows on Needs me as **Quote ready**. Open it, check it, press **Send**.
- If it says **Held**, the reason is shown. Sort that out first.

**5. The owner gets the quote** *(automatic)*
- By email and text, with a link to view it, accept it or say no.
- Reminders go on day 3 and 2 days before it expires.
- If they say no, the job closes and the installer is told.
