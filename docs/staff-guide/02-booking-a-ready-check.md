# How a Ready Check gets booked (our leads)

From "Spot saved" to a booked Ready Check. The office does **one** step; the rest happens by itself.

> **Built to be automatic, on hold for launch.** The portal is built to offer each Ready Check to an installer by itself: it goes to the approved installer covering that postcode with the fewest active jobs (then the nearest), they have 24 hours, and if they say no or don't answer it goes to the next one. After 2 misses it comes back to the office. **For launch we've switched this off and the office chooses the installer by hand** (step 2). When we switch it on, step 2 disappears.

**1. The job appears on Needs me**
- It shows as "Spot saved – waiting for an installer".

**2. You choose the installer** *(the only office step, for now)*
- Open it and press **Choose installer**.
- The portal lists nearby approved installers. Pick one.

**3. The installer gets the offer** *(automatic)*
- By text, email and in their portal app. They have 24 hours to answer.
- If they say no, or don't answer, the job comes back to Needs me and you choose again.

**4. The installer accepts by offering times** *(automatic)*
- In their portal they pick **3 times on 3 different days** (7am–5pm, within 21 days).

**5. The owner picks a time** *(automatic)*
- The owner gets an email and a text with a link, taps one of the three times, or suggests other days.
- If they suggest days, the installer and owner go back and forth until they agree. No office step.

**6. Booked** *(automatic)*
- Both get a "booked" message, and a reminder the day before.
- The Mitti A2 Ready Check form is created for the installer.
- On the day, the installer gets the 6:30am "jobs today" text with the form link.
