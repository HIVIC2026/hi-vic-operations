# What the office does

The portal does almost everything by itself. These are the only jobs that need a person. Start each day on **Needs me**: anything that needs you shows up there.

**1. Approve new trades** (Partners)
- Installers: check the business, insurance, rates, each installer's insulation certificate and White Card.
- Electricians: check the licence, REC number and their safety check rate.
- Give each new person access to Mitti.

**2. Choose the installer for our leads** (Needs me)
- When an owner saves their spot, pick a nearby installer.
- If the installer says no, or doesn't answer within 24 hours, choose again.

**3. Find an electrician** (Needs me)
- Only when no electrician takes the safety check job. Normally it goes out by itself.

**4. Check and send quotes** (Needs me → Quote ready)
- Open the quote, check it, press **Send**.
- If a quote says "Held", sort out the reason shown.
- *This stops once quote sending is switched to automatic.*

**5. Sign our part of the ESC consent form** (Needs me)
- After the owner signs it.

**6. Lodge the install notification** (VEU Registry)
- 24 hours to 5 days before the install. Type the reference into the job.
- *This stops once the Registry robot is switched on.*

**7. Final check** (Needs me)
- Look over the finished job as the AP and press **Approve**.

**8. Create the VEECs** (VEU Registry)
- *This stops once the Registry robot is switched on.*

**9. Pay the trades** (Money → Payouts)
- Pay by bank transfer when a payment shows as due, then press **Mark paid**.

**10. Follow up stuck jobs** (Needs me)
- Usually a phone call: an owner who hasn't confirmed or signed, a payment that's late, a trade who hasn't answered, a message that failed to send.

**11. Emails the portal can't place** (Needs me)
- Match the email to the right job, or ignore it.

**12. Cancel or hold a job** (the job page)
- Only when asked, and always with a reason.
