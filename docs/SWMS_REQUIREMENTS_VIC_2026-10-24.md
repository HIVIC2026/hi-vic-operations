# SWMS for VEU ceiling insulation (Activity 48), Victoria: what is actually required

Researched 24 Oct 2026 from the official texts, fetched in full:
- Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic), authorised version, regs 322–330 (legislation.vic.gov.au, 17-22sra018)
- ESC Ceiling Insulation Activity Guide v1.2 (1 Oct 2026), "Complete a safe work method statement" and "Installation requirements"
- DEECA VEU Pre-installation installer safety assessment (PIISA), Part 3 and Table 1
- WorkSafe Victoria, "Information about safe work method statements" (2023-10) and its sample SWMS template

## 1. The law: OHS Regulations 2017 (Vic)

| Reg | Requirement (official wording, shortened only where marked …) |
|---|---|
| 322 | High risk construction work (HRCW) includes, for our jobs: (a) risk of a person falling more than 2 metres; (d) removal or likely disturbance of asbestos; (f) a confined space; (l) on or near energised electrical installations or services; (q) an area where there are artificial extremes of temperature. |
| 324 | A SWMS is a document that "(a) identifies work that is high risk construction work; and (b) states the hazards and risks of that work; and (c) sufficiently describes measures to control those risks; and (d) describes how the risk control measures are to be implemented; and (e) is set out and expressed in a way that is readily accessible and comprehensible to the persons who use it." |
| 327 | It must be "prepared for the work before the work commences" and "the work is performed in accordance with the statement". If not complied with, stop the work and don't resume until it is complied with or reviewed and revised. An asbestos control plan counts as the SWMS for asbestos. |
| 328 | Review and, if necessary, revise it "whenever the high risk construction work changes" or if controls are not working, including after any incident. |
| 329 | "Keep a copy … for the duration of the high risk construction work." |
| 330 | Workers get safety training "that relates to the particular workplace". |

The OHS Act (s 35) and WorkSafe add one more requirement: affected employees must be consulted when the SWMS is prepared.

## 2. The ESC Activity Guide v1.2 (the VEU rules for this activity)

- "A safe work method statement (SWMS) must be completed when undertaking this activity. The SWMS must accurately document the appropriate steps needed to identify and manage risks for each installation."
- The risks it names ("include, but are not limited to"):
  1. exposure to live electrical hazards
  2. exposure to hazardous substances, e.g. friable asbestos, harmful chemicals
  3. exposure to biological hazards, e.g. mould, animal waste
  4. exposure to high temperatures and heat exhaustion
  5. exposure to unsafe noise and vibration levels
  6. exposure to respiratory hazards and irritants, e.g. dust, odours
  7. working in enclosed spaces
  8. working at height (both internally and externally to ceiling spaces)
  9. fire hazards and ignition sources
- "All parties involved in the installation must ensure the activity is undertaken in accordance with the completed SWMS."
- Installer must "check that all items remain compliant with the VEU pre-installation installer safety assessment form at the start of each day … (where the installation is carried out over multiple days)".

## 3. The PIISA (official form, already in our Install day form, Part 3)

- "Has a Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS) … been completed and all risks identified?"
- "Have all hazards listed in Table 1 … been considered, and control measures been implemented when developing the SWMS for this job?"
- Table 1: working at height, in roofs and ceiling (enclosed) spaces; high temperatures; electricity; hazardous noise and vibration; hazardous substances (asbestos, chemicals, solvents); respiratory and biological hazards (dust, animal sources, fibres, mould, bacteria); heat or ignition sources: flues, chimneys, gas water or space heaters, high-intensity heat lamps, exhaust fans venting into the roof space, downlights, and cables more than 400 mm surrounded by insulation.

## 4. WorkSafe guidance (recommended, not law)

- A "generic SWMS" is acceptable if it is "reviewed on site and include[s] all HRCW, taking into account site-specific hazards and risks … Any content that is not relevant to the site should be removed."
- It "should be available at the location of the HRCW … Copies can be kept in electronic format."
- "Anyone who is involved in the HRCW should sign this SWMS" after being consulted, reading it, and understanding the hazards and controls.
- The sample template asks for: the person responsible for compliance; the measures that ensure compliance (e.g. direct supervision); how the controls will be reviewed.
- "Risk matrix, risk scores are not legally required."
- Hazards not linked to HRCW (e.g. manual handling, UV) must still be managed, but don't need to be in the SWMS.

## 5. What is NOT required anywhere

No law or VEU document requires any of these in the SWMS:
- emergency details: first aider, first aid kit location, nearest hospital, assembly point, phone reception
- "prepared for" the principal contractor
- a person responsible for each separate hazard
- a risk matrix

## 6. What our SWMS therefore needs (the minimum that meets all four sources)

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| HRCW that applies (tick list) | Reg 324(a) |
| Hazards on this job, using the Activity Guide's 9 risks + Table 1 heat/ignition sources (tick list) | Reg 324(b); Activity Guide; PIISA Table 1 |
| Standard controls for each hazard, and how they're implemented: lead installer directly supervises and checks every control before first entry and each day | Reg 324(c)(d); WorkSafe template |
| Anything specific to this house, plus any change to hazards or controls during the job | WorkSafe generic-SWMS rule; Reg 328 |
| Each installer: read-and-follow tick + signature (under their name at the top) | Consultation (OHS Act s 35); WorkSafe "should sign" |
| Lead installer declaration + signature + time (before work starts) | Reg 327(1)(a) |
| Day 2+ re-check when the job runs over more than one day | Activity Guide; PIISA Part 8(i); Reg 328 |
| Copy kept (the portal files the SWMS PDF) | Reg 329 |
