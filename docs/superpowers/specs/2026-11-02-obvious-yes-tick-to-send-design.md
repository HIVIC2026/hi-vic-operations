# Obvious "yes", tick to send, free Ready Check email, normal-case names — spec (Jo, 2 Nov 2026)

## Why
Wayne (HV-2730) tapped "Yes, let's go" for Rapid, but in Choose installer he looked like any other saved spot.
Jo couldn't tell he'd said yes, couldn't pick several jobs at once, and Rapid wouldn't be told it's a free Ready Check.

## 1. Owners who said yes are obvious (In-house installers box)
- Every job in the In-house installers box where the owner said yes shows: "{Owner name} said yes · free Ready Check".
- It shows as soon as the owner taps Yes (no day-3 wait).
- Jo ticks any number (1, 2, 10…) and taps "Give {n} jobs to {Installer}". Nothing is sent before that tap.

## 2. Rapid (and every in-house installer) gets ONE email per Give
- One email to the installer's office for each Give tap, however many jobs were ticked — never one email per job.
- Subject: "New free Ready Check jobs from Home Insulation Victoria".
- Lists each job: job number, owner name, owner mobile, address.
- Says: "Call each owner to book their free Ready Check."
- Wording shown to Jo for approval before it can send (same approval rule as other trade emails).

## 3. Tick saved spots to text them (To release)
- In Choose installer › To release, Jo ticks any saved spots, picks an installer who covers all of them, taps Send.
- Each owner gets the existing confirm text/email ("confirm a few quick details"). Limit stays 10 per Send.
- Homes already texted show "Waiting for owner (sent for {Installer})" instead of a plain row.

## 4. Job page
- A job whose owner said yes shows "{Owner} said yes · free Ready Check. Give to {Installer}" with the same Give action.

## 5. Names in normal case everywhere
- Business and people names stored in ALL CAPITALS show in normal case across the portal, emails and texts
  ("RAPID INSULATION" → "Rapid Insulation"; "WAYNE WILSON" → "Wayne Wilson").
- Only names written entirely in capitals change. Mixed names (e.g. "INSolation") and short all-caps words
  inside a name (e.g. "PTY", "VIC", "ACT") stay as they are.
- Display only: stored names, ESC/legal paperwork and anything lodged keep the name exactly as stored.

## Before Rapid can use this
Rapid's in-house switch must be on. The portal refuses it until Rapid has an approved electrician (none today; Will
Casdorff of Viking Electrical was sent the sign-up link 29 Oct). Jo switches it on in Partners once he's approved.

## Not changing
Nothing goes to an owner or installer without Jo's tap. Owners' "Rapid will call" email stays as is.

To release tick-and-send texts each owner once; name formatting (all caps → normal; INSolation unchanged).
