# Quick taps: drop the roof and 5-year questions — design

Date: 3 Nov 2026 · Asked for by Jo

## Why

Mia (HV-1405, 17 Hilda Ave, Boronia) tapped "Flat" on the quick taps page and her job was closed and
she was emailed "we can't help" in one tap. The listing photo shows a normal sloped, tiled roof and she
also said there is a hatch. Jo: we can find the roof (and the house age) out ourselves, so don't ask.

## What changes

### 1. Quick taps page — the questions

Owners are asked, in this order:

1. Name (confirm or change)
2. Address (confirm or change)
3. Is there a manhole or hatch into your roof space?
4. Any website answers left blank — house type, top floor (units only), insulation
5. Who'll be at the home to let the installer in?
6. Yes, let's go / No thanks

Removed:

- "Is your roof sloped, flat, or some of each?" — never asked, so a roof answer can never close a job.
- "Was your home built, or its ceiling replaced, in the last 5 years?" — never asked.
- "Over 5 years old" as a blank website answer — never asked either, even when the website left it blank.

What still closes a job is unchanged: "No" to the hatch question, and "No thanks".

The roof and house age are checked by the installer at the Ready Check, as now. Nothing new for the office.

### 2. Old answers

Answers already saved keep their roof / 5-year values (history). The installer's job screen shows the roof
and 5-year lines only when there is an answer, so new owners just don't have them.

### 3. Mia (HV-1405) — one-off fix, after the change is live

Jo is asked again right before each live step.

1. Her "Flat" answer is set aside (marked replaced, not deleted) so her link asks her the questions again.
2. Her job goes back to "Ready Check ready to schedule", still in Rapid's batch, so the same link works.
3. She gets this text (from the normal sender, inside 7am–8pm):

   > Hi Mia, it's Home Insulation Victoria. Sorry, we think you may have tapped "flat roof" by mistake.
   > We've fixed it – if you'd still like your free Ready Check, tap here to carry on: [her link]

No other owner has been closed by a "Flat" tap (checked 3 Nov), so no one else needs this.

## Not in this change

- No office "ask again" button.
