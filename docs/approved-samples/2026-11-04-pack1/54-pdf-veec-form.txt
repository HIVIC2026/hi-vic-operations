Section 1: Consumer rights information
As the energy consumer, you own the rights to create certificates for energy saving activities undertaken at your
premises under the Victorian Energy Upgrades program. One certificate represents one tonne of carbon dioxide
equivalent (CO2-e) to be reduced by the activity.
You are able to assign your right to create certificates to an accredited provider under the Victorian Energy
Upgrades program. In assigning your right, the accredited provider will be entitled to create and own the certificates
for the assessment undertaken at your premises. In return, the accredited provider should provide you with an
identifiable benefit (e.g. price reduction on a product, free installation or a cash-back arrangement).
You are responsible for ensuring you are satisfied with the terms of the assignment of certificates to DC
Construction Solutions Pty Ltd trading as Home Insulation Victoria, the accredited provider (as detailed below) prior
to proceeding with the activity.
If you experience any issues with the outcome of this activity, you should contact DC Construction Solutions Pty Ltd
trading as Home Insulation Victoria to resolve the matter. For any outstanding issues, you can contact program staff
members at the Essential Services Commission (the commission), the government body responsible for
administering the program, by sending an email to veu@esc.vic.gov.au.
