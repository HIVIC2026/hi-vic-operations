Hazards and control measures

Task: Ceiling insulation removal (if required) and installation in the ceiling space

For every hazard below
How implemented: Lead Installer directly supervises the crew, checks the control before anyone enters the ceiling and again at the start of each day, and stops work if it cannot be followed
Responsible: John Smith (Lead Installer)
1Live electrical hazardsPower isolated, locked out and tagged before entry (solar, battery and generator too, following their instructions); PIESA read and its conditions followed; electrician on site when the PIESA requires; test for dead before touching cables.
2Hazardous substances (friable asbestos, harmful chemicals)If asbestos is suspected, do not disturb it — stop work; it is a licensed asbestos removalist's job before work continues; no chemicals or solvents used in the ceiling.
3Biological hazards (mould, animal waste)Do not disturb droppings or mould dry; P2 mask and gloves; wash hands before eating.
4High temperatures and heat exhaustionWork early, water and a break every 30 minutes, stop in extreme heat.
5Unsafe noise and vibration levelsHearing protection for blowers and power tools.
6Respiratory hazards and irritants (dust, odours)P2 mask, goggles, gloves and long sleeves in the ceiling; removed material bagged in the ceiling.
7Working in enclosed spacesExits kept clear, lighting set up first, stay in contact with someone outside the ceiling.
8Working at height (inside and outside the ceiling space)Walk only on joists or crawl boards, never on plasterboard; ladder secured and footed with three points of contact; no roof access in wind or rain.
9Fire hazards and ignition sources (flues, chimneys, gas heaters, heat lamps, exhaust fans, downlights, cables over 400 mm surrounded by insulation)AS 3999 clearances kept to flues, chimneys, gas heaters, heat lamps and exhaust fans; downlights are IC or IC‑4 rated and insulated as rated; cables over 400 mm in insulation as the PIESA directs.
