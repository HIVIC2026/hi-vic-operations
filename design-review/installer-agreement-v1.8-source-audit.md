# Installer Agreement v1.8 — commercial and Activity 48 source audit

Date: 1 October 2026  
Version: `HI-VIC-A48-INST-1.8-2026-10-01`  
Status: internal legal review required before live use

## Commercial decisions recorded

- The electrical assessment and Accredited Person management are separate owner charges of $349 inc GST each.
- The owner electrical-assessment charge remains $349 inc GST when an installer nominates its own approved REC. The REC's agreed supplier rate is internal and does not replace the owner price.
- The $220 inc GST Home Insulation Victoria lead fee applies only to Home Insulation Victoria-sourced customers. It is deducted from the installer payout only after installation and paperwork approval. It never applies to an installer's own customer.
- Staff may set the Accredited Person fee before owner acceptance. Owner acceptance locks the quoted fee.
- No generic removal amount is shown in the public calculator. Existing insulation may or may not require removal; the installer determines this at the Ready Check and any required removal is quoted separately.

## Official Activity 48 sources and retained rules

Primary source: Essential Services Commission, **Ceiling insulation activity**, including the Ceiling Insulation Activity Guide V1.2 dated 1 October 2026:  
https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/victorian-energy-upgrades/activities-offered-under-veu-program/ceiling-insulation-activity

Supporting regulation: Victorian Energy Efficiency Target (Activity 48) regulations, regulation 9:  
https://www.austlii.edu.au/au/legis/vic/num_reg/veetair2026n19o2026777/s9.html

| Source requirement | v1.8 treatment |
|---|---|
| VEU pre-installation electrical safety assessment completed and signed by the applicable licensed electrician | Retained as the formal assessment name; portal shorthand may say PIESA. |
| Assessment completed no earlier than 30 days before installation | Retained as a scheduling and progression condition. |
| Completed electrical assessment supplied to both the owner and installer at least 24 hours before installation | Retained explicitly; delivery to only one party is insufficient. |
| Separate pre-installation installer safety assessment completed by the lead installer before installation and within the 30-day window | Retained as a distinct installer obligation, not merged with the electrical assessment. |
| Minimum $200 inc GST consumer co-payment paid to the Accredited Person before VEEC creation | Retained. It cannot be reimbursed, credited back or avoided. |
| ESC may require Activity 48 records during the six years after VEEC registration | Agreement requires relevant records to remain available for this audit window; broader portal retention may be longer. |
| Electronic VEEC assignment forms are permitted and a copy must be provided within the prescribed period | Retained by reference to the approved portal assignment process and copy-delivery requirements. |

## Deliberately unresolved

The reviewed official material expressly supports electronic assignment forms but did not establish that the two safety-assessment forms may be electronically signed in every circumstance. Version 1.8 therefore does not promise a paper-only or electronic-only signing method for those safety forms. The approved current regulator form and signing method must be used.

## Consistency result

The agreement, onboarding price explanation, calculator, profitability display and compliance timeline use the same commercial rules above. The approved likely-eligible email was not changed.
